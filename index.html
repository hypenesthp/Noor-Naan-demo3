"We cook the way Bombay eats after midnight — unhurried, a little smoky, entirely without apology."

Rooted in tradition, reimagined for the modern table. Our kitchen explores the relationship between fire, spice, seasonality and memory — served across a room built for long evenings and quiet conversation.

Chef's Canvas

A tasting of the season.

"Every dish begins with a memory — usually my grandmother's kitchen, usually involving too much smoke."

CHEF ARJUN MEHTA — HEAD CHEF & PROPRIETOR

Arjun trained across Lyon and Bangkok before returning to Mumbai to build a menu around the spice trade routes of his childhood. Noor and Naan is his second restaurant, and the first built entirely around live fire.
